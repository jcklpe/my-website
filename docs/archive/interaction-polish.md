## Prototype Workflow Lessons (2026-09-23)
The retained production-artifact previews served a real diagnostic need: production rendering failed where development rendering appeared smooth, and the development toolbar's backdrop blur affected the observed behavior. Holding one compiled artifact constant allowed bounded renderer comparisons without mixing in HMR, new content, or unrelated dirty source changes. Keeping prior candidates also made regressions and GPU comparisons inspectable. This is the evidence-backed rationale for isolation; it is not a claim to recover an earlier agent's private reasoning.

The prototypes also exposed different rendering and scheduling arrangements: injected Canvas2D hooks, fixture-specific image mapping, and a redraw loop separate from application motion. These differences are experimental evidence, not automatically defects or reasons to abandon the approach. The user explicitly points out that they may reveal optimization opportunities. S55's continued-hover invalidation gap is a concrete lead to test. A later port rewrite introduced invalid GLSL, but the exact S55 fragment has since been restored in the disabled source experiment; media loading, readiness and lifecycle integration remain unverified. Neither finding warrants dismissing direct static-artifact experimentation.

Future experiments should record the baseline revision plus dirty diff, exact served artifact, changed variable, adapters, accepted behavior and evidence limits. Compare source-backed production candidates, direct static-artifact manipulation and hybrid approaches according to what they let us isolate, measure and reproduce. Keep a frozen control and preserve useful divergences as named hypotheses. Share a rendering core where useful, or mechanically compare transferred logic and document intentional differences. Validate a small end-to-end application path before calling a candidate integrated; this is an evidence boundary, not a prohibition on further standalone exploration. Keep prototype evidence, application verification and human acceptance separate.
