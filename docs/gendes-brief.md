# Generative Design Brief — gendes-desert-jackalope

## Branch

- Branch name: `gendes-desert-jackalope.cc`
- Baseline branch: `gendes-academia`
- Mood-board folder: `docs/gendes-moodboard/desert-jackalope/`
- Working title: Desert Jackalope

## Thesis

This is the warm, mythic, hand-made inverse of the cool blueprint/systems-atlas direction. The site should feel like a high-desert field guide crossed with a folk-tale broadside — sun-baked paper, gold-ink line work, woodcut critters, and the dry wit of a tall tale. The organizing spirit is the **jackalope**: a trickster, a hybrid, a naturalist's hoax that everyone half-believes. The register is mythic and naturalist but never solemn — there is a joke under the mysticism.

The twist that keeps this out of cliché territory is **a touch of color psychedelia**: against the warm earth ground, monoline desert motifs occasionally bloom into vivid gradients (blue→green, gold, red→orange), and a few feature surfaces drop to a deep jewel-dark "night desert" ground where those gradients glow. Restraint most of the time; a hit of saturated weirdness at the key moments.

This is an **exploratory demonstration run**, not a finalist for the site's final branding (the blue systems-atlas direction remains the leading candidate). Its job is to prove the generative-design method produces a genuinely distinct, coherent direction at the far pole from blue — so commit fully to the direction and make it good, but it does not need to "win."

## Palette

- **Ground/background**: warm sand/cream, pushed warmer and sandier than the academia cream — think `#f3ece0`–`#f6f0e6` range. Optional very-subtle handmade-paper grain.
- **Ink/text**: warm near-black or dark clay/umber rather than cool navy — e.g. a deep espresso-brown `#2a211a`–`#33281f`. The ink should feel sun-baked, not corporate.
- **Primary warm accents**: terracotta/clay (`~#b5582f`), ochre-gold (`~#c98a2b`), sunset coral (`~#e2683f`). Used for rules, kickers, emblem line work, links, CTAs.
- **Supporting earth tones**: sage green (`~#8a9a7b`), dusty desert-sky blue (`~#9bb0c4`). Quiet companions, not signals.
- **Psychedelic gradient family (the twist)**: blue→green, gold→amber, red→orange gradients, used on monoline illustration, an occasional rule, or a hover. Sparingly — they are the spice.
- **Jewel-dark feature ground**: a deep forest-teal or desert-midnight (`~#15302b` / `~#1c2438`) for one or two "night desert" surfaces (e.g. the Side Projects section), where gradients glow.
- **Image treatment**: warm. A subtle warm/duotone or low-contrast wash is acceptable to unify photography with the ground; full-color photos are fine if they already read warm. (This also helps the recurring slip-legibility problem — see `docs/scratch/case-hero.md`.)
- **Contrast constraints**: all body text WCAG AA on the cream ground; gradient and gold accents must not be relied on for body text contrast; verify gradient endpoints against their ground; on the jewel-dark ground, light text and gradient lines must meet AA.

## Typography

This is a deliberate departure from the mono-led blue direction.

- **Heading/display voice**: a warm humanist or transitional **serif** — letterspaced small-caps for kickers and labels (à la the Atacama board), upright for display headlines. This is the defining typographic move. (Pick a serif already loadable, or load one cleanly; if a serif can't be sourced in time, a warm slab is an acceptable fallback — but the serif is the intent.)
- **Body face**: keep IBM Plex Sans for prose, or a warm humanist sans if it improves the register. Body must stay comfortably readable.
- **Micro-labels/metadata**: IBM Plex Mono may stay for tiny technical labels (dates, counts) as a subtle nod, but it is no longer the primary voice.
- **Scale and rhythm**: editorial and airy — generous whitespace, thin rules, the boutique-studio rhythm from the "now or never" reference. Not document-tight like the blue direction.
- **What not to do**: do not default to ALL-CAPS mono everywhere; do not use a cold geometric sans; avoid a script face except possibly in a single emblem/wordmark moment.

## Surface and Material

- **Surface logic**: warm paper, not panels. Sections separated by generous whitespace and thin terracotta/gold rules rather than bordered boxes. Where a card is needed (writing/case-study cards), it is a soft framed print — thin warm border, optional faint paper grain — not a hard window-chrome panel.
- **Borders/rules**: thin 1px rules in terracotta/ochre or warm ink. A "blue-dash"-style accent has a desert analogue: a short hand-drawn dash or a fine-line motif as a section signal.
- **Texture/noise**: subtle handmade-paper grain or riso speckle on the ground; gold-ink line texture on emblems. The grain should be honest and restrained — not an obvious stock overlay.
- **Shadows/depth**: minimal and warm. Prefer flat or a very soft warm shadow over hard offset shadows. This direction is about flat printed warmth, not dimensional chrome.
- **Media framing**: featured images sit in thin warm-bordered frames or simply on the paper. No rounded-corner gallery exception is assumed — keep corners consistent with whatever the cards use.
- **Ornament (the honest-decoration opportunity)**: fine-line celestial/desert/animal motifs (sun, moon, mountains, cacti, jackrabbit, antlered jackalope) are *honest folk-art*, not BTAK — they convey the direction's identity rather than faking data. Use them as section dividers, an empty-state illustration, and an emblem. This is where the "richer visual format" energy belongs.

## Layout and Composition

- **Homepage**: warm editorial flow. The hero is a folk-broadside / field-guide plate — a warm sand panel with a fine-line or woodcut emblem (the jackalope, or a sun/mesa motif) and a serif display headline. Generous air below; sections flow on the paper.
- **Cards**: writing/case-study cards as soft framed prints — thin warm border, serif title, mono date micro-label, warm hover (border/tint warm-shift, no hard lift). A folk-line motif can mark each card subtly without labeling it.
- **Article rhythm**: keep the `.content-flow` grid. Editorial, generous, serif headings, warm rules between sections instead of cold dividers. Pull quotes feel like broadside callouts.
- **Footer/nav**: nav is quiet — serif or letterspaced small-caps, warm hover. Footer is a warm colophon, a thin terracotta top rule, an emblem, generous space.
- **The "night desert" moment**: one section (Side Projects is the natural candidate) drops to the jewel-dark ground with glowing gradient monoline art — the psychedelic peak of the page.
- **Mobile**: the editorial flow collapses cleanly to a single column; emblems and rules scale down; nothing depends on wide-only composition.
- **Composition experiments to attempt**: (1) an antlered-jackalope emblem as the identity anchor (woodcut or geometric monoline); (2) fine-line desert/celestial section dividers; (3) the gradient-monoline + jewel-dark "night desert" feature section; (4) a serif display hero on a warm field-guide plate.

## Motion and Interaction

- **Page/route motion**: keep the featured-media card-to-detail transition system intact — do not touch the `clip-path` / `data-featured-*` hooks. Otherwise motion is calm and warm.
- **Hover/touch**: warm border/tint shifts; an accent gradient may animate subtly on a key CTA. No hard lifts, no parallax.
- **Scroll behavior**: no parallax, no scroll-jacking. Content arrives on normal scroll. A gentle fade-up on section entry is acceptable if it respects reduced-motion.
- **Reduced-motion expectation**: all motion and any gradient animation must have a reduced-motion fallback; the static state must be fully legible and complete.

## Accessibility and Usability

- **Color contrast**: body text AA on the warm cream ground and on the jewel-dark ground; gradient/gold accents never carry body-text contrast alone.
- **Focus states**: keep the global `:focus-visible` outline from `_base.scss`; a terracotta/gold focus ring suits the direction.
- **Keyboard behavior**: no changes to interactive semantics — cards are links, load-more is a button, accordions keep their aria attributes.
- **Link affordances**: text links distinguishable by color *and* a non-color cue (underline or motif), not hover-only.
- **Readability**: serif display is for headings/labels; keep prose in a comfortable, well-sized reading face. Decorative line work is `aria-hidden`.

## Anti-Goals

- **Avoid generic Joshua-Tree-Airbnb / wellness-boho / yoga-studio southwest minimalism.** This is the central trap. The antidote is the jackalope's trickster wit and the psychedelic color twist — lean into specificity and weirdness, not tasteful blandness.
- **Avoid kitsch craft-fair overload** — all-woodcut, all-stencil density reads as a county-fair tea towel. Refined structure carries the folk illustration, not the reverse.
- **Avoid cold/corporate cues** — no cool navy ink, no mono-everywhere, no hard blueprint chrome (that's the other direction).
- **Avoid fake mysticism as decoration without meaning** — the desert/celestial motifs should feel like an honest field-guide/folk vocabulary, the desert version of the blue direction's BTAK rule. If a motif performs meaning it doesn't carry, cut it.
- **Avoid over-saturating the psychedelia** — gradients are the spice, not the meal. Most of the site is warm and calm; the color blooms at a few intentional moments.
